HOW TO PLAY

Master batting timing, shot angles, racing mechanics, and tournaments

🏏

1. BATTING & TIMING

Watch the ball travel towards the batsman. Time your shot as the ball hits the popping crease.

PERFECT: 6 or 4 Runs 🔥 GREAT: 3 to 4 Runs ⚡ GOOD: 1 to 2 Runs 👍 POOR: Catch Risk / 0-1 ⚠️ MISS: Bowled or Dot 💨
🏃

2. THE RACING MECHANIC

When the ball is fielded into the outfield, you must race between the wickets before the fielder throws down the stumps!

  • MASH SPACEBAR / TAP [RUN] to accelerate your batsman sprint.
  • PRESS [DIVE] (Down Arrow) near the crease for a desperate slide!
  • If the fielder fumbles, turn for a risky 2nd RUN!
🧭

3. SHOT SELECTION

Steer your shots into open gaps using Arrow Keys or on-screen directional buttons:

  • UP ARROW / LOFT: High aerial shot for a maximum SIX!
  • LEFT ARROW / OFF: Cover Drive through off-side field.
  • RIGHT ARROW / LEG: Pull & Hook shot through mid-wicket.
  • DOWN ARROW / BLOCK: Defensive block to preserve your wicket.
🏆

4. TOURNAMENT CAREER

Progress through Group Stages, qualify for Semi-Finals, and win the Grand Final to lift prestigious trophies stored in your permanent Trophy Cabinet!
