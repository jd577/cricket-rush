HOW TO PLAY

Master batting timing, shot angles, racing mechanics, and tournaments

🏏

1. BATTING & TIMING

Watch the ball travel towards the batsman. Time your shot as the ball hits the popping crease.

PERFECT: 6 or 4 Runs 🔥 GREAT: 3 to 4 Runs ⚡ GOOD: 1 to 2 Runs 👍 POOR: Catch Risk / 0-1 ⚠️ MISS: Bowled or Dot 💨
🏃

2. THE RACING MECHANIC

When the ball is fielded into the outfield you must race between the wickets before the throw shatters the stumps!

  • MASH [R] or SPACE to accelerate your sprint.
  • PRESS [D] near the crease for a desperate full-length dive.
  • PRESS [T] the moment you reach the crease to gamble on a second or third run.
  • The throw is a real physical throw — beat it home and you are safe.
🧭

3. SHOT SELECTION

Steer your shots into the gaps. Hold / tap an aim key before you swing:

  • [▲] LOFT: High aerial shot — the six ball.
  • [▶] OFF SIDE: Cover drive through the off-side field (screen right).
  • [◀] LEG SIDE: Pull & flick through midwicket (screen left).
  • [▼] BLOCK: Dead bat defence to survive the good ball.
  • [H] or SPACE: Play the shot.

Every key can be changed in Settings → Keyboard Controls.

🏆

4. TOURNAMENT CAREER

Progress through Group Stages, qualify for Semi-Finals, and win the Grand Final to lift prestigious trophies stored in your permanent Trophy Cabinet!
